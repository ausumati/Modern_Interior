08 / FAQ

Useful answers,
before the first call.

Do you handle design and execution?

Yes. The service model is presented as a comprehensive interior package, with design, technical coordination, installation and project management capabilities.

Can you work on an existing space?

Yes. Space-planning services can include rearrangement, redesign, permitted structural modifications and whole-floor or whole-home redesign.

Do you provide 3D design?

The reference material specifically describes work plans, elevations and three-dimensional images, supported by 3D and AutoCAD workflows.

Can I start with a consultation?

Yes. The recommended first step is a consultation to understand the site, requirements, design direction and execution priorities.
